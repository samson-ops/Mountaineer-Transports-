Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery locations, vehicle details and shipping dates when you request a quote, book a shipment or contact us.
  • Information collected automatically: basic device and usage data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes and arrange, schedule and complete your shipment

Who we share it with

We share the details needed to complete your shipment with the motor carrier assigned to it, and with service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer management, payment and communications systems. Text messages

If you check the consent box on our quote form, you agree to receive text messages from Mountaineer Vehicle Shipping about your quote and shipment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to keep it working and to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal requirements, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing messages, by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law.
