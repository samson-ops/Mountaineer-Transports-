FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to ship a car?

Price depends on distance, vehicle size, open or enclosed trailer, and the time of year. The quote tool at the top of this page gives you an exact all-in number for your route.

How long does shipping take?

Shorter regional routes usually take a few days once the vehicle is loaded. Coast-to-coast trips generally run about a week to ten days. We give you an estimate for your route when you book.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier we assign carries cargo insurance, and we confirm it is active before pickup. You and the driver document the vehicle's condition at both ends.

Should I choose open or enclosed transport?

Open transport is the right choice for most vehicles and costs less. Enclosed is worth it for high-value, classic or low-clearance cars that should stay out of the weather.

Can you ship a car that does not run?

Yes. Tell us in the quote form that the vehicle is not running and we will send a carrier with a winch. It needs to roll, brake and steer.
